Put the NASA C-MAPSS data files here (they are not included in this repository).

Files needed:
  train_FD001.txt   test_FD001.txt   RUL_FD001.txt
  train_FD003.txt   test_FD003.txt   RUL_FD003.txt

Where to get them:
  NASA Prognostics Center of Excellence data repository, "CMAPSS Jet Engine Simulated Data"
  (download CMAPSSData.zip and take the 6 files above out of it).
  In Google Colab the notebook asks you to upload the files (or the zip) and puts them here for you.

Licence:
  NASA lists no specific licence. The data is public and simulated, with no personal information.
  Please cite: A. Saxena, K. Goebel, D. Simon and N. Eklund, "Damage propagation modeling for
  aircraft engine run-to-failure simulation", PHM 2008.
